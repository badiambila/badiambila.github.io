move

You get two boost charges. Each jump, dodge or slam spends one, and they come back fast. Using them shows you on the enemy minimap.

SPACE ×2boost jumpGets you onto most roofs.
QdodgeA quick sidestep, on the ground or in the air.
CslamFrom the air. Land on someone and they're done.
SHIFT + CslideKeeps your speed through doors and under cover.
